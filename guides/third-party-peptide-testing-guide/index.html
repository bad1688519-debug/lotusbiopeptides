LOTUS BIO / CATALOG HELP

What to include in a testing-document request

Make the requested analytical scope explicit in your inquiry.

Name the question you need answered

Tell the team whether you require identity, purity, content or another defined test scope. A percentage alone may not answer your receiving requirements.

Identify the exact selection

Include product, SKU, specification and proposed quantity, then ask for the applicable batch or sample reference.

Verify report applicability

Check laboratory, sample reference, test methods and dates where reported. Use an original verification source when available and match it to the supplied record.

Confirm additional requirements separately

Ask about availability, timing and any additional charges before finalizing. Do not assume every test is included in a published box price.
