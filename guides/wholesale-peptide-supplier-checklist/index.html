LOTUS BIO / CATALOG HELP

Catalog inquiry checklist before confirmation

Review the information that belongs in your final business quotation.

Product and pack

Confirm exact SKU, per-vial label quantity, vials per box and number of boxes. Check alternatives separately rather than merging different strengths.

Price and shipping

Confirm currency, price unit, listed subtotal, freight and any separately quoted packaging or documentation charges. Do not assume an incomplete quote is an all-inclusive amount.

Availability and documents

Ask the team to confirm availability, preparation timing, proposed batch and applicable documents. No stock or dispatch date is guaranteed by a catalog listing alone.

Payment and issue handling

Before payment, request the confirmed payment method, quotation validity and how shortages, damage or document discrepancies should be reported. Follow the agreed order-specific terms.
