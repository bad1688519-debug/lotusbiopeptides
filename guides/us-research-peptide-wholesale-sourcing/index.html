LOTUS BIO / CATALOG HELP

Destination details for a catalog quotation

Prepare receiving information so the team can review shipping options.

Provide the receiving country first

Include country, region and postal code in your business inquiry. Do not assume a listed product price includes delivery or that every destination can be served.

Describe the receiving organization

State the organization and relevant receiving requirements. Supply detailed delivery information through your direct conversation when needed.

Ask for a separate freight line

Request shipping method, shipping cost, preparation time and estimated transit time separately. Estimates and destination requirements must be confirmed for the proposed shipment.

Keep the quotation with your records

Save the confirmed product codes, quantities, freight line and document references together. A catalog inquiry does not place or pay for an order.
