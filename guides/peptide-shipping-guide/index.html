LOTUS BIO / CATALOG HELP

Preparing a receiving checklist for a catalog order

Keep shipment and product references together for receiving review.

Before dispatch

Ask for the agreed shipment reference, pack configuration and the documents accompanying the proposed order. Confirm special receiving instructions in advance.

On receipt

Compare the delivered labels, box counts and product codes against the confirmed quotation and packing records. Record any discrepancy without substituting a similar SKU.

If packaging is damaged

Record packaging condition and retain shipment references. Contact the supply team with the facts and request handling instructions under your agreed terms.

Keep records together

Store the quotation, packing record and relevant batch documents together. Transit estimates and responsibility for charges should be confirmed before shipment.
