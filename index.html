WHOLESALE SOURCING QUESTIONS

Plan your research supply inquiry.

What does LotusBio supply?

LotusBio supports B2B inquiries for research peptide vial formats, bulk raw materials and OEM or private-label packaging. Product specifications, documentation and availability are confirmed for the proposed order.

What information is needed for a wholesale quotation?

Send the product name, catalog code or specification, quantity, destination country and packaging requirements. For vial orders, state the number of boxes; for bulk raw materials, state the required total mass.

How can buyers request a batch COA?

Request available reports through the COA page or contact the supply team. Match the product identity, batch identifier and test scope to the proposed supply batch; a report for an earlier batch does not establish results for a later batch.

Are the catalog products for human or veterinary use?

The catalog products are for laboratory and research use only, not for human or veterinary use.
