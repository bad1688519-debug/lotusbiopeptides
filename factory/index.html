CATALOG BUYER / FACTORY VIDEO REVIEW

Use factory footage to shape your sourcing questions.

These two LotusBio videos from Guangdong, China give catalog buyers a view of equipment and vial handling. Use them as a starting point for a product-specific inquiry: choose a catalog item, define its presentation and ask which current documents apply to the proposed supply.

01 / PRODUCTION ENVIRONMENT

Factory equipment & production area

The first clip shows stainless-steel industrial equipment, process piping, work platforms and the surrounding production area. When reviewing it, note the operations you want explained for your selected material. Ask which steps relate to that product and which are handled separately; the clip does not identify a particular supply batch.

02 / FILLING LINE

Vial filling-line workflow

The second clip shows operators in clean work garments handling trays of vials alongside filling-line equipment. For a vial-format inquiry, state the required amount per vial, vial count and label details. A visual view of the line does not establish measured vial contents, sterility or analytical results; those points need the relevant specification and records.

Start with the exact material

Send the product name and catalog link, required specification and total quantity. The supplier review checklist helps organize the questions behind a quote.

Match documents to the quote

Request available COA and testing records and identify the proposed batch. Confirm the product, batch reference and test scope using the quality document checklist.

Describe the presentation

Give the amount per vial, number of vials and destination. For branded labels or boxes, include your artwork needs and packaging specifications in the same inquiry.

How to use this page: The clips show a production setting and handling workflow. They do not replace a product specification, a batch report or an assessment of the process required for your material. Keep any questions raised by the footage with your inquiry so the answers can be tied to the quoted item.

TURN YOUR REVIEW INTO AN INQUIRY

Ask about the material you plan to source.

Include the catalog link, specification, vial format or bulk quantity, packaging brief and destination market. Add the documents you need and any questions about the videos. Ask our team to confirm current availability, applicable records and preparation arrangements for that inquiry.
